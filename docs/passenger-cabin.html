THE WALK THROUGH

Warmth before the platform.

Enter through the court between two offset annexes. Posters line the left wall; lockers occupy the former notice wall to the right. Coffee is served through a hatch, with a real staff workspace behind it.

Four objects worth a closer look

Select a numbered marker for the proposed inspection and first clue sequence.

Two wings, different jobs

A 5 × 5 m coffee annex and a separate 6 × 6 m restroom wing create a stepped footprint. A full-height screen and hallway hide the restroom doors from the hall. The arrival court separates food preparation from the wet rooms. The main hall extends 3.2 m for a third row of tables.

What we preserve

Central control keeps its independent keypad entrance. There is no connecting lodge door. Reach control outside via the platform; verify the annexes against the quarters, drive gallery and current site before modeling.

01 / FURNISH

Six tables. One counter.

Six 1.8 m wooden picnic tables with twelve matching benches. Twelve full-height lockers. An enclosed coffee workspace with serving hatch, prep benches and dry storage.

Coffee urn, kettle, microwave, fridge, sink and a separate hand basin support preparation, heating, washing and restocking. A public lost-property cubby sits beside the hatch.

02 / MATERIAL

Used, cared for, then left.

Warm pine, cream walls, petrol enamel, dull brass and quarry tile. Sharp gold double borders carry through to inspections. Repaired bench corners and cup rings matter more than blanket grime.

Three lightly dressed tables; three mostly clear. Leave a shelf, a locker and half the noticeboard for later evidence.
