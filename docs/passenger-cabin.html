THE WALK THROUGH

Warmth before the platform.

Enter from the sheltered corner. Lockers sit to the left; the boarding exit stays ahead. The counter occupies a side pocket, keeping queues out of the route to the gondola.

Four objects worth a closer look

Select a numbered marker for the proposed inspection and first clue sequence.

Why the L works

The rear wing gives the restrooms privacy and forms an arrival court. Shared plumbing keeps the service spaces believable. The main hall stays open, with views of the platform.

What we preserve

Central control, the upper quarters, drive gallery and the existing station routes. Final orientation and size depend on a site survey against the current meshes.

01 / FURNISH

Four tables. One counter.

Four 1.8 m wooden picnic tables with eight matching benches. Twelve full-height lockers. One coffee counter and back counter; no extra office desks.

Kettle, coffee urn, cups, snack display, shelves and a lost-property cubby give the service corner a clear purpose.

02 / MATERIAL

Used, cared for, then left.

Warm pine, cream walls, petrol enamel, dull brass and quarry tile. Sharp gold double borders carry through to inspections. Repaired bench corners and cup rings matter more than blanket grime.

Two lightly dressed tables; two mostly clear. Leave a shelf, a locker and half the noticeboard for later evidence.
