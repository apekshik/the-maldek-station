# West services: research and visual direction

Researched September 11, 2026. References inform original game meshes; they are not texture/photograph redistribution licenses. Follow the linked pages for source imagery. Historical railway references supply working arrangements, not a requirement to reproduce a British station in this alpine setting. Modern rescue and power references supply construction/function cues; omit contemporary branding, screens and GPS equipment.

## Luggage / parcels office

1. [Railway Archive: The Offices, Leicester Central](https://www.railwayarchive.org.uk/the-offices). Archival photographs and descriptions of substantial desks, paperwork trays and pigeonholes. Adopt the layered counter / sorting desk / rear storage arrangement; compress its scale for one night operator. Use paper tags and a ledger, not a computer.
2. [Cumbria Archives / Sankey: Barrow Central parcels office, image 3925](https://www.sankeyphotoarchive.uk/collection/view/?id=8960). Exterior photograph with lost-luggage identification, baskets and working doorway; adjacent catalogue record 3923 identifies the interior with ledger and luggage. Adopt legible modest signage and utilitarian handling objects. Copyright remains with the archive.
3. [Harcourt station photographic record](https://www.victorianrailways.net/photogallery/northmid/harcourt/harcourt.html). Image-search reference `harcourt_parcels.jpg` shows timber interior, shelving, parcels and operational notices. Use as a visual lead; direct full-size fetch timed out during this research. Do not claim measured dimensions from the image.
4. [Rushden Historical Transport Society: station rooms](https://www.rhts.co.uk/transport-museum/find-out-more/). Search-index text describes a sloping parcels-office floor connecting handling levels; direct page returned bot verification. This is a supporting circulation lead, not the basis for a historical accuracy claim. Our ramp geometry is an original design decision.

Design translation: aged pine shelving, petrol-painted counter base, cream enamel sign, wire secure-property cabinet, numbered removable trays. An approximately 2.2 m central handling zone must stay clear. Unclaimed belongings belong in separate tagged positions so later gameplay can change individual items.

## Rescue hut / first-aid room

5. [Moffat Mountain Rescue: equipment](https://www.moffatmrt.org.uk/what-we-do/equipment/). Rescue-team account of maintained equipment, first-aid supplies, searchlights and rigging gear with photographs. Adopt organized, checked, ready-to-use storage rather than an abandoned medical ward.
6. [Arrochar Mountain Rescue: equipment](https://www.arrocharmrt.org.uk/equipment.html). Team inventory includes stretcher, casualty bag, vacuum mattress, ropes, radios and maps. Select a small station subset: stretcher, blanket cabinet, first-aid bag, rope bag and radio point. This is an unattended aid post, not a full rescue headquarters.
7. [Mountain Rescue England and Wales / Patterdale education pack](https://www.mountainrescue.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/Education-Pack.pdf). Illustrated primary-source equipment overview. Useful for relationships between stretcher, carry handles, rope bags and team equipment; modern electronics are excluded from our period treatment.
8. [Ferno: Toboga 71 stretcher](https://ferno.it/barelle-sar-e-toboga/barella-toboga-71-unica-arancione-con-4-cinture-incluse/). Manufacturer product photography: orange rescue basket, straps, perimeter handholds and runners. Study construction; create an unbranded, period-compatible asset rather than copying product markings or making a historical model claim.

Design translation: warm cream walls, durable floor, olive wool blankets, one muted orange rescue item, folding stretcher and simple cot. Preserve room to move a 2.2 x 0.75 m stretcher through the entrance. No gore or supernatural dressing in the baseline package.

## Emergency power room

9. [Logistics Cluster: generator installation guide](https://log.logcluster.org/fr/groupes-electrogenes). Primary operational reference with generator-room cross-section and discussion of engine/alternator, installation, noise and handling. Adopt legible skid, maintenance space, intake/discharge paths and separate exhaust.
10. [Curvent: installed generator-room louvres](https://www.curvent.co.za/post/2018/07/16/louvres-lower-heat-in-generator-rooms). Installer photographs of large wall louvres. Adopt substantial framed blades and serviceable fastening, not a tiny decorative vent.
11. [Cummins: liquid-cooled generator application manual](https://powersuite.cummins.com/sites/powersuite/files/2024-04/t030.pdf). Manufacturer technical reference located in search; follow mechanical-design ventilation/exhaust sections during detail authoring. Search extracts distinguish inlet from exhaust outlet. This research does not establish an engineered size or certify the fictional room.
12. [AKMEL generator installation portfolio](https://akmel.eu/). Manufacturer/installer image-search reference shows green open generator on a skid, wall louvre, conduit and utility finishes. Useful silhouette/material lead, not a product specification for our unit.

Design translation: modest standby diesel set, mechanical meters, manual transfer selector, battery cabinet, fuel isolation, accessible filter, vibration feet and exterior exhaust riser. Emergency lights / radio / modest rescue heat only. The drive circuit is explicitly absent. Keep modern LCD panels and oversized industrial plant out of the package.

## Shared architectural synthesis

Original proposal: 6 x 10 m footprint, concrete service base and timber upper rooms under one low roof. Upper floor +4.60 m against the existing +4.00 m deck; lower floor +1.20 m. An east-facing shared porch preserves the view toward the lodge and dock. North steps give the direct player route; the south 1:12 ramp supports luggage and stretcher handling. A north outside stair reaches the lower west maintenance entrance. These dimensions are gameplay blockout targets, not source-derived dimensions or building-code claims.

Keep the lodge's established early-1990s warmth over older station infrastructure. No gratuitous second main generator: this unit gives the player a different failure-recovery job and establishes a clear rule that emergency power cannot move the gondola.
