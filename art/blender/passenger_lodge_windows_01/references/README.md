# Construction references

Accessed 10 September 2026. These are construction references for a custom visual asset, not a claim of manufacturer authorship, historical product identity or certified performance. Modern detail sheets inform the ordinary glazing construction while station proportions and finishes preserve the early-1990s design intent.

- [Kawneer Trifab VersaGlaze 451/451T architectural detail manual](https://www.kawneer.com/kawneer_files/shared%20files/97911-Arch_Manual/ADMC040EN.pdf), downloaded full PDF, January 2026 edition. Inspected page 13, **Basic Framing Details (Center - Inside Glazed - Stops Down)**: separate stops, glazing pockets, perimeter bedding and hollow profiles. Page 22/23 details were also inspected. Its 114.3 mm depth and 50.8 mm typical face provide a scale reference; PLW uses custom 110 mm depth, 70 mm perimeter and 60 mm mullions. The custom model has simplified extrusion geometry rather than copied proprietary sections.
- [Kawneer Trifab 450/451/451T installation instructions](https://www.kawneer-ecommerce.com/ipa/ResourceCenter/content/ii-sef/451VG970EN.pdf): indexed manufacturer text describes sill drainage; direct PDF returned 404 during this session. Used as corroboration only, not as an inspected drawing. The modeled top feeds and exterior outlets are independently verified in the package. Drain feeds are at 17/83% of each bay, separated from 25/75% setting blocks.
- [Pilkington Optifloat Opal](https://www.pilkington.com/en-gb/uk/products/product-categories/decoration/pilkington-optifloat-opal/): manufacturer page identifies an acid-etched, translucent product available in 4/6 mm stock, suitable for interior/exterior use and further processing. Informs the 6 mm frosted pane, softened reflection and diffused view. Blender roughness is an artistic approximation, not a measured optical property.
- [Pilkington Spacia technical explanation](https://www.pilkington.com/en/gbl/architectural-and-technical-glass/product-categories/thermal-insulation/pilkington-spacia): contrasts conventional 24 mm double glazing with thin vacuum units. Only conventional two-pane construction informs PLW's 6/12/6 mm assembly; PLW does not model a vacuum unit.
- [Steel Window Association profile drawings](https://steel-window-association.co.uk/commercial/profile-drawings/): fixed-light and multilight section families were found in the published index. Referenced for construction vocabulary only; subsequent full-page fetch timed out.
- Local approved `passenger_lodge_03/Maldek_Passenger_Lodge_Materials.blend`: primary fit/style source. Its control-room context, petrol paint, warm enamel returns and galvanized finish establish the visual relationship. Its original 50 mm solid frosted proxies are replaced with separate 6 mm panes.

Downloaded literature and temporary page renders are ignored by Git. URLs and the specific design applications are retained here so the asset can be reviewed without redistributing manufacturer manuals.
