# Manufacturer references consulted

Consulted 10 September 2026 while modeling. These inform construction and
recognizability; dimensions were adapted to the approved station envelopes.
No manufacturer mesh, texture, logo or product imagery is included in the asset.

| Reference | Applied design observations |
|---|---|
| [Bobrick traditional partition hardware](https://www.bobrick.com/products/toilet-partitions-cubicle-systems/materials-and-hardware/hardware/) | Barrel hinges, latch/keeper, visible occupancy indicator, robe hook and supporting feet/headrail. The package uses an exposed, serviceable version in the station palette. |
| [Bobrick Budget HPL partitions](https://www.bobrick.com/products/toilet-partitions-cubicle-systems/traditional-partitions/budget-hpl-series/) | Plain laminate panels and restrained metal hardware informed the stall design. |
| [Ideal Standard Tesi T0082](https://www.idealstandard.co.uk/products/t0082) | Close-coupled WC organization, separate seat and ceramic bowl/cistern. Its stated 360 x 660 x 400 mm bowl is a scale reference, not the dimensions of this custom assembly. |
| [Ideal Standard Essentials, Waverley basins and WCs](https://www.idealstandard.co.uk/dfsmedia/9e07a8a15f014cab9f19c3786cffba89/2231963-source/options/download/is-multisuite-multiproduct-bro-gb-2026essentialsbrochurelr) | Visible rim, tap deck, waste fitting, wall hangers and exposed trap; 560 x 455 mm basin and smaller handwash formats supplied size context. |
| [Kohler Dexter urinal specification](https://resources.kohler.com/onlinecatalog/pdf/116345_4.pdf) | Vitreous ceramic, projecting lower rim, backsplash and top-spud arrangement informed the refined urinal. |
| [Kohler Dexter rough-in](https://resources.kohler.com/onlinecatalog/pdf/116345_1.pdf) | Separate inlet, outlet and hangers informed the visible plumbing/mounting arrangement. |

The cream ceramic, petrol laminate and practical metalwork are an interpretation
of the lodge's early-1990s design intent. Contemporary references establish
recognizable construction, not historical product provenance. Hidden valve
internals and concealed fastening mechanisms are intentionally not modeled.
