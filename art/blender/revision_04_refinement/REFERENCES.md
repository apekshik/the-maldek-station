# Construction reference notes — 6 September 2026

These links informed original modeled components. No third-party photographs, logos, meshes or textures were imported. Existing packed Poly Haven CC0 textures remain in the source; the new enamel, rubber, instrument and galvanized fitting materials are procedural.

## Gondola

- [Doppelmayr / CWA cabins](https://www.doppelmayr.com/en/cwa-cabins/) — manufacturer context for cabin construction and glazing.
- [Vintage CWA cabin, The Gondola Shop](https://thegondolashop.com/products/44) — useful visual reference for distinct metal body, window surrounds and door hardware on an older cabin.
- Project reference: `docs/assets/reference/gondola-red-docked.png` — primary artistic target: red industrial cabin, rounded window seals, crown roof, visible hanger connection.

Interpretation: preserve the six-metre footprint and open boarding aperture; give sheet body, gasket, glass, roof lip and suspension different depths and materials. Correct the source's vertical rub strips and glass at the origin. New side windows use three repeated framed modules. Geometry radii are artistic choices, not dimensions copied from a real cabin.

## Control room

- [Rittal CX desk unit](https://www.rittal.com/com-en/products/PG20231215SCH101/PG20240214SCH001/PG20240617ZUB102/PRO136554?variantId=6710000) — sheet-steel desk enclosure and separate console components.
- [Rittal workspace solutions](https://www.rittal.com/us-en_US/products/PG20231215SCH101/PG20240214SCH001) — modular industrial control furniture.

Interpretation: replace the solid box reading with a plinth, folded housing, sloped instrument panel and front service doors. Add analog dial bezels, ticks and needles, switches and an emergency stop. Keep controls below the existing broad operator window; frame the aperture without a central mullion.

## Lower drive gallery

- [Doppelmayr reversible tramway training](https://service.doppelmayr.com/training/course-list/detail/reversible-aerial-tramway-3471/) — identifies motor, gearbox, drive pulley and braking subsystems.
- [ABB motor cooling discussion](https://new.abb.com/news/detail/108780/beat-the-heat) — separate terminal enclosure and cooling fins.
- [Calsan motor and reducer mounting base](https://www.calsanmetalurgica.com.br/projeto/base-de-motor-e-redutor/104/) — fabricator's visual reference for a motor/reducer on a shared anchored steel base.

Interpretation: distinct finned motor, terminal box, coupling, reducer, output shaft, bearing pedestal, wheel flanges and brake. Place these on bolted skids and a concrete pad. This remains a static artistic machinery study; cable reeving and a full engineered drive are outside this pass.

## Platform, grating and rail connections

- [Kee Safety Kee Klamp](https://www.keesafety.com/safety-railings/kee-klamp) — tubular members, galvanized fittings and mechanical connections.
- [Galvanizers Association: Connel Bridge walkway](https://galvanizing.org.uk/steel-structures/connel-bridge/) — exposed walkway assembled from many galvanized steel components. Useful for finish and modular assembly; it is not a mountain ropeway design.
- [Arp tramway photographic survey](https://www.remontees-mecaniques.net/bdd/reportage-tph15-d-arp-ceretti-tanfani-1213.html) — location reference for grating, concrete and open machinery in a steep alpine setting.
- [ACO S Range](https://www.aco.co.uk/s-range) — separate channel body, protective edge rails and removable grating.
- [NRMCA Concrete in Practice index](https://www.nrmca.org/association-resources/research-and-engineering/cip/) — separates joints and flatwork finishing as subjects. Its slab-on-grade guidance is not a specification for this suspended platform.

Interpretation: concrete retains a continuous simple collision surface with a panelized render surface, 12 mm dark recessed seams and broad 1.5 mm surface relief. Add a recessed service hatch and drain grating. Galvanized tubular guards keep the established run locations and receive base plates, sockets, collars and hex fasteners. Exposed grating gets steel toe edges. No metal rivets are scattered into the concrete itself.

## Audit findings before editing

1. Gondola glass meshes are at the origin; rub strips extend vertically far beyond the cabin. Correct these before adding detail.
2. A single strongly streaked material dominates cabin, console and machinery. Separate their material responses and reduce oversized bump.
3. The flywheel intersects the motor placeholder; components need a legible connection chain.
4. Platform reads as a continuous reflective sheet; introduce actual joints, restrained relief, recessed service pieces and railing attachments.

Audit PNGs are isolated Cycles material studies, not viewport screenshots. The same camera, studio lights, exposure and render settings are used for each before/after pair. Night comparisons retain the saved scene lighting separately.
