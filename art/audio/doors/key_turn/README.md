# Recorded cylinder turn

**Key Twist in lock** by **KieranKeegan**, recorded with a Zoom h2n.

- Source: https://freesound.org/people/KieranKeegan/sounds/418846/
- License: CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Downloaded the site's public high-quality MP3 preview on 2026-09-08.

`originals/418846.mp3` is the downloaded recording. `prepare.py` makes the game-ready `KeyTurn.wav` using mono conversion, resampling, gain and tiny edge fades. It retains the complete recording and original pitch; there is no synthesis or generated layer. `source.json` records provenance, hashes and audio levels.

The one-shot starts at full insertion, accompanying the cylinder's turn and return. The existing separate release click plays when the interaction completes. Cancellation stops the turn recording. `KeyTurnSound` is assigned only to the seven physical-key doors; keypads retain their recorded button and bolt sounds.
