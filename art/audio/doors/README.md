# Door and keypad sound design

Seven original, deterministic sound-design effects generated by prepare.py (NumPy, 48 kHz mono 16-bit PCM). No external recordings or samples are used. The keypad combines a short tactile click with restrained electronic tones; clear descends, acceptance rises, and rejection uses two low tones. Door sounds combine metallic latch transients, a quiet continuous hinge/friction loop, and a resonant closing impact. These are designed effects, not recordings of a real steel door.

Each native StationDoor owns separate spatial event and movement components. Effects attenuate over 11 metres beyond a 1-metre inner radius. The motion loop follows actual angular movement and stops at rest or when blocked; the closing impact fires only when the leaf reaches its shut position. Both inward and outward swings are supported. Keyboard and mouse input use the same keypad sound paths.

manifest.json retains source peaks, RMS and durations. wav/Audition.wav sequences the original effects for listening; only the seven individual effects are imported into Unreal. Existing station ambience and player sound banks are untouched.
